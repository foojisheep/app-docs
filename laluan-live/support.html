Support

Need help with LaluanLive? Start here, or reach us directly at support@foooverse.com.

Is LaluanLive an official RapidKL app?

No. LaluanLive is an independent, community project. It combines official RapidKL notices with reports from riders to give a fuller picture of what's happening on a line, but it isn't affiliated with RapidKL. For time-critical decisions, always check official announcements and station staff.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can view line statuses and submit reports without signing up. Your device gets an anonymous ID automatically, used only to apply fair limits like reporting caps — it isn't linked to your name or other personal details.

Do I have to attach a photo to submit a report?

No — photos and notes aren't collected anymore. Just pick the problem, optionally choose the station, and send it. Older reports that already had a photo or a note still show it.

How long is my report kept?

Reports and the incident they belong to are deleted 48 hours after the issue is marked resolved. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What does the "Agree" button do?

Tapping Agree lets you confirm a rider report matches what you're seeing too. It's a "me too" signal shown as a count on the report — it doesn't change the line's status by itself, and you can withdraw it any time. You can't agree with your own report, or with one that's already resolved.

What does "Running fine" do?

Tapping it on a line's page tells other riders things seem normal right now. It needs no note and doesn't show up in the feed. When three different riders tap it, an open rider report on that line closes. You can use it once per line per hour.

Where do the official notices come from?

From the service announcements RapidKL publishes on its website, which LaluanLive checks automatically every 10 minutes at peak times (5–10 am and 4–9 pm), every 30 minutes at other times and every 2 hours overnight. Each notice is shown with a short English title, such as "Train delay: Kelana Jaya Line · Gombak". A notice about several lines shows on each of them, and a named station is marked on the line's page; tap "View official notice" for RapidKL's full announcement in Malay and English. Notices can lag behind what's happening on the platform, so for time-critical decisions check RapidKL directly. When RapidKL announces service has been restored, the earlier open items for that line close. RapidKL's general updates also appear, titled "Update"; they're for information only and never change a line's status or send alerts.

What does "Last checked RapidKL … Updates may be delayed" mean?

LaluanLive checks RapidKL's announcements every 10 minutes at peak times (5–10 am and 4–9 pm), every 30 minutes at other times and every 2 hours overnight. If two scheduled checks in a row don't go through, this message appears so that a quiet board isn't mistaken for "all lines running normally". It's on our side, not your phone or connection, and there's nothing you need to do. Rider reports keep working the whole time, and the message clears by itself on the next successful check. For anything urgent, check RapidKL directly.

How do I get alerts when my line has a problem?

Open your line's page and tap the bell, then allow notifications. You'll get an alert when that line becomes delayed, disrupted or halted, and when it's back to normal, even when LaluanLive is closed. You can follow several lines. On iPhone or iPad, add LaluanLive to your Home Screen first (in Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen), open it from there, then tap the bell. This needs iOS 16.4 or later.

I turned on alerts but I'm not getting any. What should I check?

  • Nothing has changed yet. Alerts only come when a line you follow changes status, which on a good day may be never. They can arrive up to about 5 minutes after the change.
  • The bell is on. Open the line's page and check the bell is filled in.
  • iPhone: you must use LaluanLive from your Home Screen, not a Safari tab, and be on iOS 16.4 or later. In Settings → Notifications → LaluanLive, make sure Allow Notifications is on.
  • Focus or Do Not Disturb may be holding alerts back. They follow your phone's own settings.
  • Android or a computer: check that notifications are allowed for the LaluanLive site in your browser's settings.
  • If you blocked notifications earlier, allow them again in your phone or browser settings, then tap the bell once more. If you removed LaluanLive from your Home Screen, add it again and turn the bell back on.

How do I stop alerts?

Tap the bell again on each line you follow. Once no line is followed, your alert subscription is deleted. You can also turn off notifications for LaluanLive in your phone's settings at any time.

Why can't I see the "Running fine" button?

It's shown on every line's page. Tapping it always registers as a confirmation, but it only has an effect on a line's open reports while that line is affected — on a line already running normally, there's nothing for it to help close.

Why don't I see the "Need another way?" section?

It only appears while a line is delayed, disrupted or halted, so on a line running normally it's hidden. When it's shown, it lists live MRT feeder buses near the affected station (where there are any), RapidKL's alternative service details when they're available, a link to open Google Maps at that station, and a link to Grab's website to book a ride. Most RapidKL notices only reach LaluanLive as a short title, so alternative service details are often missing; tap "View official notice" for the full announcement. LaluanLive doesn't plan routes itself and doesn't know your location or destination.

How does a rider report go away?

It closes on its own once the line has been quiet for 30 minutes, or sooner when three different riders tap "Running fine" on that line. It also closes when RapidKL announces that service is back. Reports can't be flagged or removed by other riders, and reporting is never blocked for a device; the only limits are one report per line every 5 minutes and up to 6 an hour.

Can I remove a report I submitted?

Reports are cleaned up automatically per our retention schedule. If you need something removed sooner, contact us at support@foooverse.com with as much detail as possible (line, station, approximate time) and we'll assist.

I found a bug or something isn't working. What should I do?

Please email us at support@foooverse.com with:

  • A short description of what happened
  • What you expected to happen instead
  • The device/browser you were using, if known

How do I request my data be deleted?

Since LaluanLive doesn't collect personal information or require an account, there is typically nothing tied to you personally to delete beyond your own reports, which are removed automatically per our retention policy. If you turned on alerts, turning off the bell on every line deletes your alert subscription. If you have a specific concern, email us and we'll help.

Can I suggest a new feature or line to add?

Yes — we welcome feedback. Send suggestions to support@foooverse.com.
