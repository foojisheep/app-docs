Support

Need help with LaluanLive? Start here, or reach us directly at support@foooverse.com.

Is LaluanLive an official RapidKL app?

No. LaluanLive is an independent, community project. It combines official RapidKL notices with reports from riders to give a fuller picture of what's happening on a line, but it isn't affiliated with RapidKL. For time-critical decisions, always check official announcements and station staff.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can view line statuses and submit reports without signing up. Your device gets an anonymous ID automatically, used only to apply fair limits like flagging and reporting caps — it isn't linked to your name or other personal details.

Do I have to attach a photo to submit a report?

No — photos aren't collected anymore. Just pick the problem, optionally add the station and a short note, and send it. Older reports that already had a photo still show it.

How long is my report kept?

Reports and the incident they belong to are deleted 48 hours after the issue is marked resolved. News items are deleted 48 hours after publication. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What does the "Agree" button do?

Tapping Agree lets you confirm a rider report matches what you're seeing too. It's a "me too" signal shown as a count on the report — it doesn't change the line's status by itself, and you can withdraw it any time. You can't agree with your own report, or with one that's already resolved.

What does "Running fine" do?

Tapping it on a line's page tells other riders things seem normal right now. It needs no note, doesn't show up in the feed, and can't be flagged. Enough "Running fine" taps can help an open rider report close sooner. You can use it once per line per hour.

Where do the official notices come from?

From RapidKL's own service announcements, published in Malay and English — LaluanLive shows the English version. Notices can lag behind what's happening on the platform, so for time-critical decisions check RapidKL directly. When RapidKL announces service has been restored, the earlier open items for that line close.

Why can't I see the "Running fine" button?

It's shown on every line's page. Tapping it always registers as a confirmation, but it only has an effect on a line's open reports while that line is affected — on a line already running normally, there's nothing for it to help close.

What is the alternatives section on a line's page?

When a line is affected, its page can show alternative service details from RapidKL's own notice (when it mentions any), a link to open Google Maps at the affected station, and a link to find a ride nearby. LaluanLive doesn't plan routes itself and doesn't know your location or destination, so those links hand you over to other services.

Someone flagged my report and it disappeared — why?

If enough different riders flag a report as incorrect, unrelated, spam, or sensitive, it's removed automatically. This keeps the feed reliable for everyone. If you believe your report was flagged unfairly, contact us and we'll take a look.

Reporting seems to be disabled for me — what happened?

If 5 of your reports have been removed through flagging, reporting and confirming "Running fine" are disabled on that device to keep the feed trustworthy. You can still view everything else normally. If you think this was a mistake, email us and we'll review it.

Can I remove a report I submitted?

Reports are cleaned up automatically per our retention schedule. If you need something removed sooner, contact us at support@foooverse.com with as much detail as possible (line, station, approximate time) and we'll assist.

I found a bug or something isn't working. What should I do?

Please email us at support@foooverse.com with:

  • A short description of what happened
  • What you expected to happen instead
  • The device/browser you were using, if known

How do I request my data be deleted?

Since LaluanLive doesn't collect personal information or require an account, there is typically nothing tied to you personally to delete beyond your own reports, which are removed automatically per our retention policy. If you have a specific concern, email us and we'll help.

Can I suggest a new feature or line to add?

Yes — we welcome feedback. Send suggestions to support@foooverse.com.

For anything not covered above, reach out anytime.
