# Support

Need help with **RailPulse**? Start here, or reach us directly at **support@foooverse.com**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is RailPulse an official rail operator app?**
No. RailPulse is an independent, community-driven project. It combines official operator notices with reports from riders to give a fuller picture of what's happening on a line, but it is not affiliated with any rail operator. For time-critical decisions, always check official announcements and station staff.

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. You can view line statuses and submit reports without signing up. An anonymous identifier is generated automatically on your device so we can apply fair limits (like report flagging), but it's never linked to your name or other personal details.

**Why do I have to attach a photo to submit a report?**
Photos help other riders trust that a report is genuine and give useful visual context. It also helps reduce spam and fake reports.

**How long is my report kept?**
Reports and photos are kept only while the related issue is relevant, and for a limited time after it's marked resolved. After that, they're permanently and automatically deleted. See our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) for details.

**Someone flagged my report and it disappeared — why?**
If enough different riders flag a report as incorrect, unrelated, spam, or containing sensitive content, it's automatically removed. This keeps the feed reliable for everyone. If you believe your report was flagged unfairly, contact us and we'll take a look.

**Reporting seems to be disabled for me — what happened?**
If several of your reports have been removed through community flagging, reporting may be temporarily restricted on your device to keep the feed trustworthy. You can still view all statuses and reports normally. If you think this was a mistake, email us and we'll review it.

**Can I remove a report I submitted?**
Reports are automatically cleaned up according to our retention schedule. If you need something removed sooner — for example, you shared a photo by mistake — contact us at support@foooverse.com with as much detail as possible (line, station, approximate time) and we'll assist.

**I found a bug or something isn't working. What should I do?**
Please email us at support@foooverse.com with:
- A short description of what happened
- What you expected to happen instead
- The device/browser you were using, if known

**How do I request my data be deleted?**
Since RailPulse doesn't collect personal information or require an account, there is typically nothing tied to you personally to delete beyond your own reports and photos, which are removed automatically per our retention policy. If you have a specific concern, email us and we'll help.

**Can I suggest a new feature or line to add?**
Yes — we welcome feedback. Send suggestions to support@foooverse.com.
