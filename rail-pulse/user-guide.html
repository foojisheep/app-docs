User Guide

Welcome to RailPulse — a community-powered way to check live status and report issues on rail lines, brought to you by Foooverse.

Getting Started

No sign-up is required. Simply open the App and you'll see the current status of every tracked line.

Checking Line Status

The main screen shows every line with a color-coded status:

  • Green — Normal service
  • Yellow — Delayed
  • Orange/Red — Disrupted
  • Dark red — Service halted

Tap any line to see its full list of stations and recent reports specific to that line.

Refreshing for the Latest Updates

  • Pull down on the screen to refresh and check for new reports.
  • Or tap the refresh icon at the top of the screen at any time.

Refreshing does not reopen the information panel — see below for when that appears.

Reading a Report

Each report in the feed shows:

  • Where it came from — an official operator notice, a community discussion post, or a fellow rider.
  • What's happening, and which station it relates to.
  • When it was posted.
  • A photo, when one was included.
Official notices may also include an expected repair or resume time, shown as a small highlighted note (for example, "Maintenance window: tonight 12:30am–4:30am" or "Targeted completion: [date]"), along with a link so you can read the original notice in full.

Reporting an Issue

If you notice something happening on a line — a delay, a breakdown, crowding, or anything else — you can let other riders know:

  1. Tap the Report button.
  2. Select the line and station.
  3. Choose the issue type.
  4. Add a short note describing what's happening.
  5. Attach a photo. A photo is required to submit a report — this helps other riders trust what they're seeing.
  6. Tap Submit.

Your report will appear in the feed for other riders to see. Please avoid including faces, license plates, or anything else you wouldn't want shown publicly — your photo is visible to anyone viewing that line.

Flagging a Report

If you see a report that looks wrong, unrelated, spam, or contains sensitive content, you can flag it:

  1. Tap Flag on the report.
  2. Choose the reason that best fits.

Reports flagged by enough different riders are automatically removed. Flags are final and cannot be undone once submitted, so please only flag reports that genuinely need it.

About Your Data

  • No account or personal information is required to use the App.
  • Reports and photos are automatically deleted a set time after the related issue is marked resolved.
  • A short explanation of how your data is handled appears each time you open the App — tap the info icon at any time to view it again.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

If Reporting Is Disabled on Your Device

If several of your reports are removed by other riders for being incorrect or inappropriate, reporting may be temporarily disabled on your device. You can still view all line statuses and reports as normal. If you believe this happened in error, contact us at support@foooverse.com.

Switching Between Light and Dark Mode

Tap the sun/moon icon at the top of the screen to switch between light and dark themes at any time.

Getting Help

If you run into a problem or have feedback, see our Support page or email support@foooverse.com.
