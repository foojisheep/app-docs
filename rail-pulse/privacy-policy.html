Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

RailPulse (the "App") is operated by Foooverse ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, how we use it, and how long we keep it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require an account, sign-up, or personal details to use the App. The information we handle falls into these categories:

  • Anonymous device identifier. When you first open the App, a random identifier is generated and stored on your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, phone number, email, or any other personal detail. It exists solely to tell reports and flags submitted by different devices apart, and to apply fair limits (see Section 4).
  • Reports you submit. When you report an issue on a line or station, we collect the line/station selected, the issue type, any note you write, and a required photo.
  • Photos. A photo is required to submit a report. Photos are visible to other riders viewing that line and are automatically deleted along with the rest of your report (see Section 3).
  • Flags. If you flag another rider's report, we record which device flagged it and the reason selected.
  • Basic usage data. Like most apps, our hosting and analytics providers may automatically log standard technical information (such as approximate region, browser/device type, and timestamps of requests) for security and performance purposes. We do not use this to identify you personally.

2. What We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for or knowingly collect your name, email address, phone number, exact location history, or any government-issued identification. We do not require you to create an account to view line statuses or submit reports.

3. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Reports and their photos are kept only for as long as the related issue is active, and for 48 hours after it is marked resolved. After that, the report, its photo, and related flags are permanently deleted.
  • Aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example, how often a line experiences disruptions) may be retained indefinitely. These summaries contain no photos, notes, or device identifiers — only counts and dates.
  • If your device is restricted from submitting reports due to repeated flagged content (see our Terms of Service), we retain a record of that restriction tied to your anonymous device identifier only, so we can enforce it consistently.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Show accurate, up-to-date line and station statuses to all users.
  • Let riders submit and view reports and supporting photos.
  • Apply fair, consistent moderation (flagging and removal of inappropriate or incorrect reports).
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the App.

We do not sell your information. We do not use your reports or photos for advertising or profiling.

5. Sharing of Information

  • Other users of the App can see reports, photos, and notes you submit, along with your report's line and station — never any personal identity, since none is collected.
  • Service providers who host the App, store photos, and provide infrastructure may process this information strictly on our behalf and under confidentiality obligations, solely to operate the App.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process.

6. Your Photo

Because your photo is visible to other riders once submitted, please avoid including faces, license plates, or any other content you would not want to be seen publicly. We are not able to manually review every photo before it appears; instead we rely on rider flagging (see our Terms of Service) to remove inappropriate content quickly.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has submitted content to the App, please contact us using the details below and we will remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request removal of content, please contact us at:
