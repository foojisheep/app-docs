Sprint Verdict for Jira

Is this amount of work actually possible for these people, in these two weeks? A sprint board doesn't answer that. This does.

Try it without installing anything

The live demo is the real app running on sample data. Every page works, and nothing is connected to Jira — change someone's days off and watch the capacity maths move.

Nothing leaves Atlassian

No database, no servers, no third parties. The app runs inside Atlassian's Forge platform, and team settings are stored as a property on your own Jira project — so the data stays yours, and stays put if you uninstall.

What it does

  • Current sprint — committed against real capacity, per person, with leave and holidays taken off
  • Planning — what isn't ready, how much runway is left, what fits next sprint, and capacity in the sprints ahead
  • Delivery trends — velocity, predictability, and a per-person delivery heatmap
  • Portfolio — several teams side by side, with people counted once even when they work on two boards
  • Any estimation field — story points, dev days, t-shirt sizes, hours, or plain issue count

Get started

Installing it? Start with the documentation. Reading the numbers? The user guide explains what they mean. If something isn't working, see support.
