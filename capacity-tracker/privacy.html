Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

The short version

This app does not store your data on any server we control. It has no database, no analytics, and no external services. Everything it reads and writes stays inside your own Atlassian Cloud site.

What the app reads

Only what it needs to calculate capacity, and never more than the person using it could already see. Jira is read by the app rather than as the individual, so the app enforces the check itself: before returning anything it asks Jira which projects that viewer may browse, and drops every board they may not. A board it cannot match to a project is hidden rather than shown.

The app does not read issue descriptions or comments except to check whether a description exists at all (a backlog-readiness rule). The content is never stored or transmitted.

What the app writes

One thing, and only when someone with project admin permission saves it on the Team page:

  • A Jira project property named capacity-tracker.team on the project you're viewing

It holds one team per board in that project. For each board: the roster (display name and Atlassian account ID), per-person availability settings, the leave-source configuration, any leave typed in by hand, and the public holiday calendar.

Holidays can be added in bulk by pasting a list. That is still one write to the same property — a year of holidays is a single save, not one per date.

The app never creates, edits, transitions, comments on or deletes an issue, sprint, board or project.

Leave read from Jira issues is never copied into this property. It stays where it is.

The last row is the only thing the app stores outside Jira, and it is two strings — which theme you picked and whether you prefer light, dark or automatic. It never leaves your browser, is never sent to Jira or to us, and two people looking at the same board can have different settings. Clearing your browser data resets it and loses nothing else.

Nothing is stored on infrastructure operated by Foooverse. The app runs entirely inside Atlassian's Forge platform.

What we do not do

  • We do not run any server, database or data warehouse
  • We do not transmit your data outside Atlassian's infrastructure
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels or session recording
  • We do not use your data to train machine learning models
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone
  • We do not place advertising in the app
  • We do not combine data across customers. The Portfolio page reads several of your boards in one request and shows them side by side; nothing is aggregated across Jira sites, organisations or customers, because the app has nowhere to aggregate it to.

Personal data

The only personal data the app handles is what Jira already holds: display names and Atlassian account IDs of people on a board, plus their leave dates if you configure leave tracking.

Leave dates can imply sensitive information — a long absence recorded as sick leave, for example. The app reads leave you have already chosen to record in Jira, applies your own project permissions to it, and stores none of it. If that inference concerns you, use a generic leave type or keep leave in a project with restricted permissions.

We are a data processor; you remain the data controller.

Retention and deletion

  • Project properties persist until you delete them or delete the project
  • To remove everything the app has written: DELETE /rest/api/3/project/{KEY}/properties/capacity-tracker.team
  • Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage. The project property survives by design, so your configuration isn't lost if you reinstall. Delete it with the call above if you want it gone.

Sub-processors

None. The app has no external network access. Atlassian hosts the runtime and the data.

Data residency

The app stores no data of its own, so residency is entirely determined by your Atlassian Cloud site's region. Forge respects Atlassian's data residency commitments.

Your rights

Because we hold none of your data, requests for access, correction, export or deletion are served directly from your Jira using the REST calls above. Contact us at foooverse@gmail.com if you need help.
